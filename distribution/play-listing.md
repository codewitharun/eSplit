## Full description
EzySplit makes shared money simple. Split bills with friends, flatmates and family. Track your own spending too, and settle up in a couple of taps.

SPLIT ANY WAY YOU LIKE
• Split equally, by exact amounts, by percentage or by shares
• Multi-item bills: add each item and its price, and EzySplit adds up the total
• Edit history on every expense, so changes are always visible

KNOW WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Personal spend lists for expenses that are just yours
• Group insights: total spend, your share and this month vs last month
• Activity grouped by day, with a clear "you lent / you owe" on every expense
• Multi-currency groups for trips abroad

SETTLE UP WITHOUT THE AWKWARDNESS
• Live balances: see who owes whom at a glance
• Settle up with UPI in a tap, and record cash or other payments
• Every settlement is tracked, so balances stay accurate

GROUPS THAT STAY PRIVATE
• Invite with a link, a 6-character code or a QR code
• Admins approve join requests, so only people you know get in
• Lock a group anytime to stop new members joining

ASK EZYSPLIT (AI ASSISTANT)
Ask questions about your spending in plain language, like "How much did I spend on food this month?" The answers come from your own EzySplit data. Rolling out gradually.

EXPORT & STAY IN THE LOOP
• Export any group as a PDF report or an Excel sheet
• Notifications when expenses are added or someone joins

DESIGNED FOR EVERY DAY
A fast, modern dark interface with smooth animations and haptics, rebuilt from the ground up.

Free to use. Sign in with Google and start splitting in seconds.
